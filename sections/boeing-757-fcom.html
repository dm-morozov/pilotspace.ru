Примеры вопросов

Несколько вопросов из этого раздела. Выбор ответа и проверка доступны в тренировке.

  1. Which of the following EICAS messages will cause the beeper to sound?

    • A. Caution messages.
    • B. Advisory messages.

  2. How many hydraulic systems are installed on the 757?

    • A. Two.
    • B. Three.

  3. Where are the packs located?

    • A. Aft fuselage, by the APU.
    • B. Lower fuselage around the main gear wells.

  4. Where is the APU ground Fire Control Panel?

    • A. Nose Gear strut right side.
    • B. Right Main Gear strut.

  5. Where are the inlet and exhaust areas for the air conditioning packs?

    • A. Lower fuselage around the main gear wells.
    • B. Aft fuselage, by the APU.
