Руководство полетов по EDTO (ETOPS): вопросы и ответы

EDTO (ETOPS): вопросы планирования полёта и выбора запасных аэродромов.

Ответы приведены по тестовой базе MY FREIGHTER с задокументированными исправлениями. Это материал для повторения, а не самостоятельное руководство по эксплуатации. Правовые вопросы включают нормы Узбекистана. Об источнике. Ссылки на полный PDF требуют интернета.

Вопросы раздела

1Select correct definition of EDTO:
Ответ по ключу базы
  • A. Any operations by an aeroplane with two or more turbine engines where the diversion time to an en-route alternate aerodrome is greater than the threshold time established by the State of the Operator.
Источник: PDF, стр. 461 ↗
2What kind of approval must be obtained by operator to conduct EDTO flights?
Ответ по ключу базы
  • B. Approval for Extended diversion time operations issued by CAA.
Источник: PDF, стр. 461 ↗
3It is allowed to exceed the runway PCN (Pavement Classification Number), when an airport is used in case of emergency.
Ответ по ключу базы
  • A. True.
Источник: PDF, стр. 461 ↗
4An aerodrome is considered adequate for EDTO planning when it has been assessed to meet fundamental considerations other than weather, including required runway length and an available approach procedure.
Ответ по ключу базы
  • A. True.
Источник: PDF, стр. 461 ↗
5Adequate aerodromes used for EDTO planning are generally listed in the approved operator’s manual, and the required ETOPS/EDTO en-route alternate aerodrome(s) for a specific flight shall be specified in the operational flight plan and ATS flight plan.
Ответ по ключу базы
  • A. True.
Источник: PDF, стр. 461 ↗
6What kinds of ground operational assistance are mandatory for adequate airport?
Ответ по ключу базы
  • C. ATC, sufficient lighting, communications, weather reporting, Navaids, and emergency services.
Источник: PDF, стр. 461 ↗
7What kinds of landing facilities (navaids) are mandatory for adequate airport?
Ответ по ключу базы
  • C. At least one instrument approach procedure available for an instrument approach
Источник: PDF, стр. 461 ↗
8What validity period must be considered for the forecast weather conditions when nominating an ETOPS/EDTO en-route alternate aerodrome?
Ответ по ключу базы
  • A. The period commencing at the earliest potential time of landing and ending 1 hour after the latest nominated time of use of that aerodrome.
Источник: PDF, стр. 462 ↗
9What is a parameter included necessarily to company EDTO approval?
Ответ по ключу базы
  • B. Maximum diversion time.
Источник: PDF, стр. 462 ↗
10What Maximum EDTO diversion time for MY FREIGHTER (Boeing 767)?
Ответ по ключу базы
  • C. 120 min.
Источник: PDF, стр. 462 ↗
11Actual diversion time in flight may be higher than the diversion time used to establish the EDTO area of operation, provided that the planned route remains within the applicable still-air range from an en- route alternate aerodrome.
Ответ по ключу базы
  • A. True.
Источник: PDF, стр. 462 ↗
12Maximum Diversion Distance is used for:
Ответ по ключу базы
  • C. Dimensioning of the area of operations.
Источник: PDF, стр. 462 ↗
13EDTO Area of Operations:
Ответ по ключу базы
  • B. Represented by the area enclosed within arcs/circles centered on the selected EDTO en-route alternate aerodromes, using the approved maximum diversion distance.
Источник: PDF, стр. 462 ↗
14EDTO Entry Point (EEP) is:
Ответ по ключу базы
  • A. The first point on the route that is beyond the applicable EDTO threshold time/distance from an en-route alternate aerodrome.
Источник: PDF, стр. 462 ↗
15An EDTO route may have multiple EDTO sectors, separated by non-EDTO segments.
Ответ по ключу базы
  • A. True.
Источник: PDF, стр. 463 ↗
16What is a point on the route located at the same flying time from two selected EDTO en-route alternate aerodromes?
Ответ по ключу базы
  • B. ETP.
Источник: PDF, стр. 463 ↗
17When one of ETPs (usually last on the route) can become critical point (CP)?
Ответ по ключу базы
  • B. When the carriage of additional EDTO fuel reserves is required, because fuel difference between the standard fuel expected to be on board (fuel to continue) and the required EDTO diversion fuel (fuel to divert) is minimum or negative.
Источник: PDF, стр. 463 ↗
18One-engine-inoperative diversion speed is used for:
Ответ по ключу базы
  • A. Defining the maximum diversion distance / area of operation.
Источник: PDF, стр. 463 ↗
19The size of EDTO area of operations depends on:
Ответ по ключу базы
  • A. The maximum diversion range, the one-engine-inoperative diversion speed, and the number and location of the selected EDTO en-route alternate aerodromes.
Источник: PDF, стр. 463 ↗
20What is a difference between standard and EDTO fuel calculations?
Ответ по ключу базы
  • C. Defining of additional fuel (critical fuel reserve), if this fuel reserve is required for particular flight.
Источник: PDF, стр. 463 ↗
21How are ETOPS/EDTO en-route alternate aerodrome planning minima determined?
Ответ по ключу базы
  • A. By adding the applicable additional limits to the published operating minima for the available approach procedure.
Источник: PDF, стр. 463 ↗
22The EDTO dispatch planning minima apply until dispatch.
Ответ по ключу базы
  • A. True.
Источник: PDF, стр. 463 ↗
23What are the crew procedures necessary to conduct additionally during EDTO flight?
Ответ по ключу базы
  • B. Aircraft systems monitoring; weather monitoring; fuel monitoring, diversion planning with respect to all operational considerations.
Источник: PDF, стр. 464 ↗
24If the engine failure occurred during EDTO segment, what operational factors must be considered by the CPT during decision-making?
Ответ по ключу базы
  • C. Closest adequate airport is preferable, but actual weather situation (alternate and en-route) and all another operational factors must be considered.
Источник: PDF, стр. 464 ↗
25Select the correct decision in case of impossibility to meet EDTO dispatch requirements before particular flight:
Ответ по ключу базы
  • B. Select another route, aircraft or delay the flight until all EDTO dispatch requirements are met and required procedures are completed.
Источник: PDF, стр. 464 ↗
26According to ICAO 10085, what additional limits are added to the operating minima for a non- precision approach or circling approach when determining ETOPS en-route alternate planning minima?
Ответ по ключу базы
  • A. Authorised MDH/MDA plus 400 ft and authorised visibility plus 1500 m.
Источник: PDF, стр. 464 ↗
27According to ICAO 10085, what additional limits are added to the operating minima for precision approach when determining ETOPS en-route alternate planning minima?
Ответ по ключу базы
  • A. Authorised DH/DA plus 200 ft and authorised visibility plus 800 m.
Источник: PDF, стр. 464 ↗
28In the event of a diversion on an EDTO flight, the flight crew is not obligated to divert to the primary EDTO alternate aerodrome listed on the flight plan and may choose a different aerodrome as the nearest suitable landing site if this is deemed to be a safer course of action based on prevailing operational conditions.
Ответ по ключу базы
  • A. True
Источник: PDF, стр. 464 ↗
29An aeroplane should not be released on an EDTO flight unless the air operator’s operations manual and/or as applicable the EDTO flight operations manual (EFOM) contain(s) sufficient performance data to support all phases of any applicable EDTO operation, including flight preparation and en-route operations
Ответ по ключу базы
  • A. True
Источник: PDF, стр. 464 ↗
30What should be verified/completed before dispatching an ETOPS/EDTO flight?
Ответ по ключу базы
  • A. Aircraft EDTO serviceability/status including applicable MEL/CDL restrictions, ETOPS/EDTO en-route alternate selection and weather, critical fuel planning, and required flight documentation.
Источник: PDF, стр. 465 ↗
31According to ICAO Doc 10085, which ICAO provisions does the EDTO Manual support?
Ответ по ключу базы
  • A. Annex 6 - Operation of Aircraft, Part I, Section 4.7 and Attachment C.
Источник: PDF, стр. 465 ↗
32What must be verified before proceeding beyond the EDTO Entry Point (EEP)?
Ответ по ключу базы
  • A. The forecast weather at the EDTO alternate aerodromes identified on the OFP is equal to or exceeds the published operating minima for the expected runway and approach procedure during the applicable validity period.
Источник: PDF, стр. 465 ↗
33For an aircraft with either 120-minute or 180-minute maximum approved diversion time, an operator may request an increase in the operator’s approved diversion time for specific routes, provided that the requested diversion time does not exceed either 115% of the maximum approved diversion time or the time-limited system capability minus 15 minutes.
Ответ по ключу базы
  • A. True
Источник: PDF, стр. 465 ↗
34Which documentation should be provided to the flight crew before an ETOPS/EDTO flight?
Ответ по ключу базы
  • A. NOTAMs, weather forecasts/reports, OFP, ATS flight plan with required ETOPS/EDTO en-route alternates, and relevant route/EDTO chart or plotting chart.
Источник: PDF, стр. 465 ↗
35An extension of the operator’s approved diversion time up to 115% of the maximum approved diversion time:
Ответ по ключу базы
  • B. Must take into account the aeroplane’s most limiting time-limited system capability, including cargo fire suppression capability where applicable.
Источник: PDF, стр. 465 ↗
36The operator’s approved diversion time is an operational limit that should not exceed either: the maximum approved diversion time, or the time-limited system capability minus 15 minutes.
Ответ по ключу базы
  • A. True
Источник: PDF, стр. 465 ↗
37Choose the correct statement:
Ответ по ключу базы
  • A. The 60-minute area of operation and associated 60-minute threshold distance calculation is normally based on a High Engine Inoperative speed up to the maximum permissible operating speed or Mach number (VMO/MMO according FCOM limitations) in order to maximize the area of operation.
Источник: PDF, стр. 466 ↗
