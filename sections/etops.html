Руководство полетов по EDTO (ETOPS)

EDTO (ETOPS): вопросы планирования полёта и выбора запасных аэродромов.

Изучить вопросы и ответыВыбрать тренировку →

Примеры вопросов

Несколько вопросов из этого раздела. Выбор ответа и проверка доступны в тренировке.

  1. Select correct definition of EDTO:

    • A. Any operations by an aeroplane with two or more turbine engines where the diversion time to an en-route alternate aerodrome is greater than the threshold time established by the State of the Operator.
    • B. Any operations over a route that contains a point further than 60 minutes flying time from an adequate airport at the selected one-engine-out diversion speed schedule in still air and ISA conditions.
    • C. Any flight over water on a twin-engine ETOPS designed aircraft.

  2. What kind of approval must be obtained by operator to conduct EDTO flights?

    • A. Approval for Extended diversion time operations issued by Head of company.
    • B. Approval for Extended diversion time operations issued by CAA.
    • C. Approval for Extended diversion time operations issued by Aircraft Manufacturer

  3. It is allowed to exceed the runway PCN (Pavement Classification Number), when an airport is used in case of emergency.

    • A. True.
    • B. False.

  4. An aerodrome is considered adequate for EDTO planning when it has been assessed to meet fundamental considerations other than weather, including required runway length and an available approach procedure.

    • A. True.
    • B. False.

  5. Adequate aerodromes used for EDTO planning are generally listed in the approved operator’s manual, and the required ETOPS/EDTO en-route alternate aerodrome(s) for a specific flight shall be specified in the operational flight plan and ATS flight plan.

    • A. True.
    • B. False.

Как заниматься

Выберите 10, 20 или 50 вопросов либо свой диапазон. Прогресс сохраняется в браузере, а неверные ответы попадают в повторение. Для занятий без сети заранее скачайте пакет на главной странице.
